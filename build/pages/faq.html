FAQ

Straight answers to common questions.

Including when the honest answer is that you don't need us. If your question isn't here, a technical lead will answer it on a call.

Ownership and contracts

Do we own the code you write?

Yes. Intellectual property transfers to you as each milestone is paid, and the code lives in your own repository from the first commit.

Will you sign an NDA before we talk?

Yes. Tick the NDA box on the contact form and we will send a mutual NDA before the first call.

How do you handle data protection?

We sign data processing agreements as standard, work in your cloud accounts where possible, and follow the Kenya Data Protection Act, GDPR and any other laws that apply to you.

Pricing and payment

How do you price projects?

Every engagement starts with a short, fixed-price discovery phase. From there we quote a fixed price for a defined scope, or a monthly rate for a dedicated team. See pricing for details.

How accurate is the online estimator?

It uses the same rates we quote with, and most final proposals land within its range. It can't see your existing systems, which is what discovery is for.

Which currencies do you invoice in?

US dollars, Kenyan shillings, euros or pounds. Fixed-scope work is paid by milestone; team and support plans are invoiced monthly.

Working together

Can you take over a project another team started?

Often, yes. We start with a two-week code and infrastructure audit, then give you an honest recommendation: continue, refactor, or rebuild specific parts.

Do you work with clients outside Kenya?

Yes. About a third of our clients are in other parts of Africa, Europe and the Middle East. We overlap with UK and EU working hours every day.

How involved do we need to be?

Plan for one person on your side who can make decisions, about two to four hours a week, plus attending the fortnightly demo.

After launch

What happens after launch?

Every project includes 30 days of free fixes. After that most clients move to a support retainer, but you are free to take the work in-house and we will run the handover.

Do you support systems you didn't build?

Yes, after a short audit so we know what we are taking on. Around a quarter of our support clients came to us this way.
