How we work

Working software every two weeks. No surprises at the end.

The same five stages for a six-week integration or a year-long platform build. Each one ends with something you can see, use and approve before we move on.

  1. 01

    Discovery

    1 to 2 weeks · fixed price

    We map your current systems, interview the people who use them, and agree on what success looks like in numbers.

    • Process and systems map
    • Prioritised backlog
    • Fixed quote for the build
  2. 02

    Design

    2 to 3 weeks

    Architecture, data model and clickable prototypes, tested with real users and reviewed with your team before production code.

    • Clickable prototype
    • Architecture decision record
    • Delivery plan with milestones
  3. 03

    Build

    Varies by scope

    Two-week sprints with a demo at the end of each. You have access to a staging site and the code repository from day one.

    • Fortnightly demos
    • Staging environment
    • Weekly written update
  4. 04

    Launch

    1 to 2 weeks

    Load testing, data migration, staff training and a planned cut-over with a rollback ready if anything goes wrong.

    • Launch runbook
    • Staff training sessions
    • 30 days of free fixes
  5. 05

    Support

    Ongoing, optional

    Monitoring, security patches and a monthly block of hours for improvements. Or a full handover if you are taking it in-house.

    • Uptime and error monitoring
    • Monthly health report
    • 4-hour critical response
