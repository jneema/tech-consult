About Corbel

A small senior team that builds things to last.

We started in 2015 as three engineers who kept being asked to fix systems other vendors had abandoned. Eleven years later we are 34 people, and we still measure ourselves by how long our software stays in use.

Why "Corbel"

A corbel is the bracket that sticks out of a wall to hold up a beam. Nobody notices it, but take it away and the structure falls down.

That is the kind of work we like: the integration that keeps finance and operations in sync, the platform that quietly processes thousands of loan applications, the infrastructure that means nobody sleeps next to a server during exam season.

We are independent and engineer-led. There are no account managers between you and the people writing your code, and every project lead has shipped the kind of system you need before.

How we work

Four commitments we make on every project, and the reason clients come back.

01

Plain language, early

You hear about risks and delays the day we spot them, in words that don't need a translator.

02

Working software every two weeks

Demos, not status reports. You can click through progress on a staging site from the first sprint.

03

Built for whoever comes next

Documentation, tests and boring, well-known technology, so your team or another vendor can carry on without us.

04

Honest about fit

If an off-the-shelf tool will do the job, we will tell you, even when it means a smaller project for us.

Leadership

The people who will lead your project. Every one of them still writes code or designs every week.
