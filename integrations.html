What we deliver

Most integration projects include one or more of these. Each is scoped and priced separately so you only pay for what you need.

Payments and mobile money

M-Pesa, Airtel Money, card and bank integrations with automatic reconciliation and refunds.

CRM and ERP sync

Two-way sync between Salesforce, HubSpot, SAP Business One, Odoo, QuickBooks and Xero.

APIs and middleware

Well-documented APIs and integration layers with queues, retries and idempotency built in.

Monitoring and alerting

Dashboards and alerts for every integration, so a failed sync is fixed in minutes rather than found at month end.

A good fit if

  • Staff copy data between systems by hand every day
  • Finance and operations disagree on the numbers
  • You are adding a new tool and need it to work with the rest
  • An existing integration fails silently and nobody notices until month end

How a typical project runs

Timings are typical for this kind of work. Your proposal will set out exact dates. See our full process.

  1. Systems audit

    We document every system, the data that moves between them, and where it breaks today.

    1 week

  2. Integration design

    Data mapping, error handling and a test plan, agreed with finance and operations.

    1 week

  3. Build and test

    Built against sandbox accounts, then tested with real data in a controlled rollout.

    2 to 6 weeks

  4. Monitor

    Dashboards and alerts go live with the integration, plus a runbook for your team.

    Ongoing

What if the other system doesn't have an API?

Who maintains the integration after launch?

Your choice. We hand over documentation and runbooks, or keep it running under a support retainer with alerting.

Can you work with our existing vendors?

Yes. We regularly coordinate with ERP partners and payment providers on your behalf.
